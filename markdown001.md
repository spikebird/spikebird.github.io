## Topic: Elements of a Short Story
These notes cover key elements commonly discussed in English class when studying short stories.

### Key Points
- Short stories focus on a single event or small set of events.
- They typically include essential story elements such as characters, setting, and theme.
- The plot usually follows a traditional structure: exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, and resolution.

---

##  Vocabulary
1. Protagonist: The main character in the story.
2. Antagonist: A character or force that opposes the protagonist.
3. Setting: The time and location where the story happens.
4. Theme: The central message or lesson of the story.
5. Conflict: The main problem or struggle faced by the protagonist.
